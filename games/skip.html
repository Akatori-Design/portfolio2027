How to play
Skip it far
  1. Tap on the flashThe stone flashes yellow as it skids. Tap before the flash fades.
  2. Later is flatterA late tap skims flat and fast. Wait too long and it sinks.
  3. Chain good skipsEvery good tap in a row adds speed and height.
Watch out for
  1. Logs and ducksHit one and you lose speed and your streak. Hop over, or tap early to drop short.
  2. BranchesStay low and flat to slip underneath.
